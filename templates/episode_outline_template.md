## Episode Structure

### 1. Cold Open / Hook

- Opening image, question, scene, or tension.
- Why the listener should care immediately.

### 2. Context Setup

- Historical, social, personal, scientific, or business context.
- Minimum background needed for the audience.

### 3. Main Character / Event / Central Question

- Introduce the main person, event, conflict, or idea.
- Clarify the episode’s central question.

### 4. Background and Stakes

- What was happening before the main event?
- What was at risk?

### 5. Rising Conflict or Complexity

- Add complications, contradictions, decisions, or tensions.

### 6. Turning Point

- The moment where the story shifts.

### 7. Consequences

- What changed afterward?
- Who was affected?

### 8. Reflection / Analysis

- What does this story mean?
- Why does it matter now?

### 9. Closing

- Emotional, analytical, or reflective ending.
- Optional final line with resonance.

### 10. CTA

- Optional: subscribe, share, comment, read more, visit website, etc.
